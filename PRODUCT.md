# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers, engineers, and small product teams (2–20 people) who need a project management tool without enterprise bloat. They live in their IDE and terminal all day. They reach for FlowBoard between code commits — they want speed and clarity, not onboarding tutorials.

Secondary: freelancers and agency owners who manage client projects and need to present professional task tracking to clients.

## Product Purpose

FlowBoard is a focused, fast project management tool in the Linear / Jira family. It exists as a portfolio project demonstrating full-stack Next.js + Prisma engineering for Upwork clients. The product covers: Kanban boards, sprints, list view, calendar, Gantt, analytics, activity feed, AI chat, global search, and my-tasks — all in one dark-first app shell.

Success: a visitor lands on a live demo and immediately recognizes "this is a Jira/Linear caliber tool built by a serious engineer."

## Brand Personality

Focused, sharp, fast. The product gets out of your way. It surfaces exactly what you need, hides the rest. Every interaction should feel deliberate and snappy.

## Anti-references

- **Old Jira**: heavy, cluttered, enterprise-grey, 12 levels of nested menus.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: hero-metric cards, gradient blobs, identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- **Trello / Asana**: colorful sticker-board consumer energy, cute iconography.
- **Monday.com**: rainbow columns, gamified progress bars, visual overload.

## Design Principles

1. **Density without clutter.** Show more in less space — but every element must earn its pixel. Remove decoration that doesn't carry meaning.
2. **Structure over fluidity.** Toolbars are toolbars, not flex-wrapped button soups. Hierarchy comes from fixed chrome, not from emergence.
3. **Actions where you look.** Put controls at the point of intent: back navigation on sub-pages, edit in context, status inline with the task.
4. **Respect the data model.** Jira-style task IDs (KEY-N) are canonical identifiers. Show them consistently everywhere and make them navigable links, not decorative labels.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Long AI outputs, metadata panels, and secondary sections collapse by default. Expand on demand with clear affordance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Dark mode primary; light mode supported. Focus-visible on all interactive elements. Keyboard navigation for all primary flows.
